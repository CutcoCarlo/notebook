How to use Notebook

  • Write and draw with the Apple Pencil using Pen or Highlighter. Press harder for a thicker line.
  • Pencil only (bottom right) means fingers never draw: one finger scrolls, two fingers pinch to zoom. Turn it off to draw with a finger.
  • Eraser removes whole strokes you touch.
  • Select: tap something to select it, or draw a loop around several things. Once selected, drag it to move, pull a corner to resize, use the top handle to rotate. The small toolbar above the selection changes color, duplicates or deletes.
  • Boxes, circles, lines, arrows: pick the tool and drag. Tap once for a default size. They stay editable.
  • Fill (paint bucket): pick a color, then tap inside any closed area, whether it was drawn by hand, with boxes and lines, or a mix. Small gaps are closed for you; if the colour spreads to the page edge the area wasn’t closed, so undo, close the gap, and try again. Tap a filled area again with a new colour to recolour it. Fills sit underneath your ink, and you can select and delete them.
  • Snap (bottom right) makes line ends, bends and shape corners jump to the corners, ends, midpoints and centres of nearby shapes, and lines boxes up edge‑to‑edge while you move them. A pink ring or guide line shows what it snapped to. Turn it off for free placement.
  • Curves: select a line or arrow and drag the small + dot in its middle to bend it. Each bend is a blue handle you can keep dragging; more + dots appear on every segment for S‑curves. Double‑tap a blue handle to remove it, or tap Straighten.
  • Text: pick Text and tap the page. Type, or write in the box with the Pencil and the iPad turns it into typed text (Scribble). Double‑tap a text box later to edit it.
  • Photo and Link insert in the middle of the view. Select a link and tap Open to visit it.
  • Pages: use ‹ › at the top, + Page to add one, or tap the page number to open the page list. There you can move a page with ▲ ▼ (or hold a thumbnail and drag it), duplicate it, copy it to another notebook, or delete it.
  • Export from the ⋯ menu. Choose Google Drive in the share sheet to send a PDF to Claude.
  • Everything is saved on this device automatically and works with no internet. Add the app to your Home Screen from Safari’s Share menu.
